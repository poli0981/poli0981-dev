The first night watching a render, the case fan hummed steadily — a heartbeat on loan. I didn't
expect a three-minute video to eat this much time, but that's the price of wanting everything
**just right**.

## Why "muted"

SkullMute isn't the absence of sound. It's sound turned down **on purpose** — keeping only what's
worth keeping. This site is the same: dark, warm, and bright in exactly one place.

## What I learned

- Exporting `AV1` takes twice as long but is much lighter.
- Sleep earlier; don't babysit the progress bar.
- A hot cup of tea beats ten refreshes.
